**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Iskan Nrxakan app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Mumin (collectively, the "Application").

**Information We Collect**

Our service providers may process your IP address, request timestamps, and technical request details to authenticate sessions, deliver rate data, secure services, and troubleshoot. The Application does not use analytics to track pages viewed or time spent and does not collect GPS location.

**Authentication and Account Information**

The Application uses Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple to authenticate users. When you sign in with either provider, we receive your email address and a provider-specific account identifier to create and manage your account. With Sign in with Apple, you may choose to hide your email address; Apple may then provide a private relay email address. The Application does not collect GPS location data.

**Premium and Purchase Information**

For Apple purchases, Apple and RevenueCat process purchase and subscription information to verify and manage Premium access. We identify your RevenueCat customer using your Supabase account ID and store verified subscription status in Supabase. We do not receive your payment card or bank account details. If you contact us on WhatsApp, your account reference appears in a draft message and is sent only if you choose to send it. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction records under their policies; contact us for purchase-data deletion requests.

**Account Deletion and Data Management**

You can request account deletion at any time:
1. In the app: Go to Settings > "Delete Account & Data", type DELETE, and confirm. The app reports completion only after the server confirms the deletion.
2. By email: Send a request from your registered email address to muminrtx@gmail.com. We will process the request within 30 days.
To prevent a deleted account from being recreated, we retain a restricted record of its email address for that purpose; it cannot be used to sign in.

**Third Party Access**

We use Supabase to authenticate users and store account and request data. Cloudflare provides public currency and gold-rate data to the Application. If you choose to send a WhatsApp message, WhatsApp and our support team can see its contents. We do not use personal data for advertising or sell it.

**Data Retention**

Active account data is deleted when an account deletion request is completed. We may retain a restricted email record to prevent the deleted account from being recreated, and may retain information where required by law.

**Children**

The Application is not intended for children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

**Security**

We provide physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect information we process and maintain.

**Changes**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy with an effective date.
